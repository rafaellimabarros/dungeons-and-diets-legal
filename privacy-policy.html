Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Dungeons & Diets ("the App", "We", "Us", or "Our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

  • Email address (used for login and account recovery)
  • Username and hero name (chosen by you during setup)

1.2 Health & Fitness Data

To provide the core features of the App, we collect health-related information that you voluntarily enter:

  • Body weight and weight history
  • Body measurements (waist, hips, chest, arms, and other body parts)
  • Body fat percentage
  • Dietary goals (weight loss or weight gain target)
  • Progress photos (uploaded voluntarily by you)
  • Meal logs and dietary plans created by you

This data is stored on your account and is only accessible by you. We do not share health data with third parties for advertising or profiling purposes.

1.3 App Usage Data

We collect data generated by your use of the App to power its gamification features:

  • Check-in history, streaks, XP, and in-app coins
  • Achievements unlocked and items purchased in the in-app store
  • Weekly progress reports

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the App: store your progress, display stats, and enable all features.
  • To manage your account: authenticate your login and allow account recovery.
  • To improve the App: understand how features are used and fix issues.
  • To respond to your requests: answer support questions sent via email.

We do not use your data for advertising, sell it to third parties, or share it with data brokers.

3. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

Your data is stored using Supabase (database, authentication, and file storage). Supabase is our only data infrastructure provider. You can review Supabase's privacy practices at supabase.com/privacy.

Data may be stored on servers located outside Brazil. We rely on Supabase's security standards to protect data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest (AES-256 encryption).

We do not use third-party advertising networks, social login providers, or analytics SDKs that transmit personal data externally.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time directly from the App:

Settings → Delete Account

Deletion removes all your personal data, health records, photos, meal plans, and progress history from our systems. Residual copies in encrypted backups are purged within 30 days.

If you prefer to request deletion by email, contact us at rafaellimainfolink@gmail.com and we will process the request within 15 business days.

5. Your Rights (LGPD – Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados)

Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD – Law 13,709/2018), you have the right to:

  • Confirm the existence of processing of your personal data
  • Access your personal data
  • Correct incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated data
  • Request anonymization, blocking, or deletion of unnecessary or excessive data
  • Request the portability of your data
  • Obtain information about third parties with whom data is shared
  • Revoke consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at rafaellimainfolink@gmail.com.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal data, contact us immediately and we will delete it.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and encrypted storage. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
